## 5. Data Retention

### 5.1 Active Accounts

We retain your data while your account is active.

### 5.2 Closed Accounts

After account closure:
- **Immediately deleted**: Login credentials
- **30 days**: Personal contact information
- **1 year**: Transaction history (tax/legal requirements)
- **3 years**: Reviews and ratings (platform integrity)
- **7 years**: Financial records (legal requirements)

### 5.3 Legal Holds

Data may be retained longer if:
- Required by law
- Subject to legal proceedings
- Necessary for dispute resolution
