# Q-MÉTIER Refund Policy

**Last Updated**: February 28, 2026  
**Effective Date**: February 28, 2026

## 1. Overview

This Refund Policy explains when and how refunds are issued for credit pack purchases on Q-MÉTIER.

**Key Point**: Credits are **virtual tokens**, not money. Refunds are limited and subject to strict conditions.

---

## 2. General Refund Policy

### 2.1 NO REFUNDS (Standard Policy)

**Credit purchases are FINAL and NON-REFUNDABLE** except in the specific circumstances listed below.

Once credits are added to your account:
- ❌ No refunds for unused credits
- ❌ No refunds if you change your mind
- ❌ No refunds if you don't get jobs
- ❌ No refunds if quotes are rejected
- ❌ No refunds if projects are cancelled
- ❌ No refunds if you close your account

**Why No Refunds?**
- Credits are digital goods delivered instantly
- You receive full value immediately
- Credits don't expire
- You can use them anytime

---

## 3. Exceptions (When Refunds ARE Issued)

### 3.1 Technical Errors

**Full Refund** if:
- Payment processed but credits NOT added
- Duplicate charge (charged twice)
- Wrong credit amount delivered
- System error during purchase

**How to Request**:
- Email: support@qmetier.ca
- Subject: "Technical Error Refund"
- Include: Transaction ID, screenshot, description
- **Timeframe**: Within 48 hours of purchase

**Processing Time**: 5-10 business days

### 3.2 Unauthorized Charges

**Full Refund** if:
- Your account was hacked
- Someone used your payment method without permission
- Fraudulent transaction

**How to Request**:
- Email: support@qmetier.ca
- Subject: "Unauthorized Charge"
- Include: Transaction ID, police report (if filed)
- **Timeframe**: Within 30 days of charge

**Investigation**: We will investigate and may require:
- Proof of unauthorized access
- Police report
- Affidavit of fraud

**Processing Time**: 10-15 business days after investigation

### 3.3 Service Unavailability

**Partial Refund** if:
- Platform is down for 7+ consecutive days
- You cannot access your account due to our error
- Critical features are unavailable

**Refund Amount**: Prorated based on downtime

**How to Request**:
- Email: support@qmetier.ca
- Subject: "Service Unavailability Refund"
- **Timeframe**: Within 14 days of issue

**Processing Time**: 10-15 business days

### 3.4 Account Termination by Q-MÉTIER

**Partial Refund** if:
- We terminate your account without cause
- We ban you by mistake

**Refund Amount**: Unused credits only (prorated)

**NOT Refunded** if:
- You violated Terms of Service
- You engaged in fraud
- You harassed other users
- You were banned for cause

**Processing Time**: 15-20 business days

## 5. Refund Process

### 5.1 How to Request a Refund

**Step 1**: Email support@qmetier.ca  
**Step 2**: Include:
- Your name and email
- Transaction ID (from Stripe receipt)
- Purchase date and amount
- Reason for refund request
- Supporting documentation (screenshots, etc.)

**Step 3**: Wait for response (2-3 business days)

**Step 4**: If approved, refund processed to original payment method

### 5.2 Refund Methods

**Original Payment Method**:
- Credit card: 5-10 business days
- Debit card: 5-10 business days
- Bank account: 7-14 business days

**We Do NOT Refund**:
- To different payment methods
- In cash
- As platform credits (unless you request)
- To third parties

### 5.3 Refund Timeline

| Step | Timeframe |

## 6. Chargebacks

### 6.1 Chargeback Policy

**DO NOT file a chargeback** without contacting us first.

If you file a chargeback:
- Your account will be suspended immediately
- All credits will be frozen
- We will contest the chargeback
- You may be banned permanently

**Why?** Chargebacks cost us fees and are often fraudulent.

### 6.2 Legitimate Chargebacks

If you have a legitimate dispute:
1. Contact us first: support@qmetier.ca
2. Give us 10 business days to resolve
3. If unresolved, then file chargeback

We will NOT penalize legitimate disputes.

### 6.3 Chargeback Consequences

**If Chargeback is Upheld** (you win):
- Account remains suspended
- Credits are removed
- You may create new account after 90 days

**If Chargeback is Denied** (we win):
- You owe us the chargeback fee ($25)
- Account remains banned
- Legal action may be taken

## 10. Partial Refunds

### 10.1 When Issued

Partial refunds may be issued for:
- Service outages (prorated)
- Account termination by us (unused credits only)
- Goodwill gestures (rare, at our discretion)

### 10.2 Calculation

**Formula**: (Unused Credits ÷ Total Credits) × Purchase Price

**Example**:
- Purchased: 24 credits for $34.99
- Used: 10 credits
- Unused: 14 credits
- Refund: (14 ÷ 24) × $34.99 = $20.41

### 10.3 Minimum Refund

We do not issue refunds under $5.00.
