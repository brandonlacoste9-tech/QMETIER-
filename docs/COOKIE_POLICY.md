# Q-MÉTIER Cookie Policy

**Last Updated**: February 28, 2026

## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your experience.

---

## 2. Cookies We Use

### 2.1 Essential Cookies (Required)

**Purpose**: Make the platform work  
**Examples**:
- Session management (keep you logged in)
- Security (prevent fraud)
- Load balancing (distribute traffic)

**Can You Disable?** No - platform won't work without them

### 2.2 Analytics Cookies (Optional)

**Purpose**: Understand how you use the platform  
**Provider**: Google Analytics  
**Data Collected**:
- Pages visited
- Time on site
- Browser type
- Device type
- Anonymized IP address

**Can You Disable?** Yes - in your account settings

### 2.3 Preference Cookies (Optional)

**Purpose**: Remember your choices  
**Examples**:
- Language preference (EN/FR)
- Location settings
- UI customization

**Can You Disable?** Yes - but you'll need to reset preferences each visit

---

## 4. Managing Cookies

### 4.1 Browser Settings

**Chrome**: Settings → Privacy → Cookies  
**Firefox**: Options → Privacy → Cookies  
**Safari**: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies  
**Edge**: Settings → Privacy → Cookies

### 4.2 Our Platform

**Account Settings** → **Privacy** → **Cookie Preferences**

### 4.3 Opt-Out Tools

- Browser extensions (Privacy Badger, uBlock Origin)
- Do Not Track browser setting (we respect it)

---

## 5. Cookie Lifespan

| Cookie Type | Duration |
|-------------|----------|
| Session | Until you log out |
| Remember Me | 30 days |
| Analytics | 2 years |
| Preferences | 1 year |
