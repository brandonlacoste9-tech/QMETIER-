# Q-MÉTIER Acceptable Use Policy

**Last Updated**: February 28, 2026

## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines prohibited conduct on Q-MÉTIER. Violations may result in account suspension or termination.

---

## 2. Prohibited Activities

### 2.1 Illegal Activities
- ❌ Any illegal activity under Canadian law
- ❌ Fraud or misrepresentation
- ❌ Money laundering
- ❌ Tax evasion
- ❌ Unlicensed professional services (where license required)

### 2.2 Harassment & Abuse
- ❌ Harassment, threats, or intimidation
- ❌ Hate speech or discrimination
- ❌ Sexual harassment
- ❌ Bullying or stalking
- ❌ Doxxing (publishing private information)

### 2.3 Fraud & Scams
- ❌ Fake profiles or impersonation
- ❌ False reviews or ratings
- ❌ Bid manipulation
- ❌ Payment fraud
- ❌ Identity theft
- ❌ Phishing attempts

### 2.4 Platform Abuse
- ❌ Circumventing credit system
- ❌ Creating multiple accounts to abuse promotions
- ❌ Automated scraping or bots
- ❌ Reverse engineering platform
- ❌ Exploiting bugs for gain

### 2.5 Spam & Solicitation
- ❌ Spam messages
- ❌ Unsolicited marketing
- ❌ Off-platform solicitation
- ❌ Pyramid schemes or MLM
- ❌ Phishing links


### 2.6 Intellectual Property Violations
- ❌ Copyright infringement
- ❌ Trademark violations
- ❌ Using others' photos without permission
- ❌ Plagiarism

### 2.7 Inappropriate Content
- ❌ Pornography or sexual content
- ❌ Violence or gore
- ❌ Illegal drugs or weapons
- ❌ Hate symbols
- ❌ Graphic content

---

## 3. Professional Conduct

### 3.1 Professionals Must
- ✅ Provide accurate qualifications
- ✅ Honor quoted prices
- ✅ Complete work as agreed
- ✅ Maintain proper licensing/insurance
- ✅ Communicate professionally

### 3.2 Professionals Must NOT
- ❌ Misrepresent skills or experience
- ❌ Abandon projects without notice
- ❌ Demand payment outside agreed terms
- ❌ Perform unlicensed work
- ❌ Subcontract without disclosure

---

## 4. Customer Conduct

### 4.1 Customers Must
- ✅ Provide accurate project descriptions
- ✅ Pay professionals as agreed
- ✅ Communicate respectfully
- ✅ Leave honest reviews

### 4.2 Customers Must NOT
- ❌ Post fake projects
- ❌ Refuse payment for completed work
- ❌ Leave false reviews
- ❌ Harass professionals
- ❌ Demand free work

---

## 5. Consequences

### 5.1 Warning
First offense (minor): Written warning

### 5.2 Suspension
Repeated or serious violations: 7-30 day suspension

### 5.3 Termination
Severe violations: Permanent ban, no refund

### 5.4 Legal Action
Criminal activity: Reported to authorities

---

## 6. Reporting Violations

**Email**: abuse@qmetier.ca  
**Include**: User ID, description, evidence (screenshots)

---

## 7. Appeals

If you believe you were banned unfairly:
**Email**: appeals@qmetier.ca  
**Response**: 10 business days
